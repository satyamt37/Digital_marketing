03 — Demand

Meta & Google campaigns, on a three-kilometre leash

A national campaign is useless to a shop in one locality. Everything we run is fenced to the streets people will actually cross for you.

Radius targeting

Ads shown to people within walking or short-driving distance, split by area so we can see which colony actually converts.

Weekly optimisation

Every Monday: kill the losers, scale the winners, launch three fresh variations. No campaign is left running on autopilot.

Your money, your account

Ad spend is billed by Google and Meta to your own card. We take no commission on it and never mark it up.

Campaign types we run

  • Walk-in campaigns — "get directions" and store-visit objectives
  • Lead forms — for clinics, gyms, coaching and real estate
  • Click-to-WhatsApp — the highest-converting format for most Indian shops
  • Google Search — for "near me" and category intent
  • Retargeting — the people who watched but didn't act

What a sensible budget looks like

For one shop, ₹8,000–₹15,000 a month in ad spend is the honest starting range. Below ₹5,000 the platforms never gather enough data to get efficient, and you end up paying for the learning without reaching the payoff.

  • We tell you when spending more stops helping
  • Budget split visible to you at all times
  • Pause any time — it's your account
04 — Discovery

Local SEO & your Google profile

The free channel almost every shop is sitting on and almost nobody maintains.

When somebody within a kilometre searches your category, three businesses appear in the map pack. Getting into those three is mostly a matter of doing dull things consistently: correct categories, fresh photos, weekly posts, answered questions, and a steady trickle of new reviews.

  • Profile claimed, verified and correctly categorised
  • Fresh photos uploaded every week, geo-tagged
  • Offers and events posted so the listing never looks abandoned
  • Questions answered before a competitor answers them for you
  • Listings kept consistent across Justdial, Maps and Apple Maps
