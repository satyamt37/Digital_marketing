Nine shops that stopped being a well-kept secret

Every one of these started the same way — good business, no online presence worth the name. What changed was consistency, not luck.

Featured — Diwali 2025

Sharma Sweets & Namkeen

Sweets & namkeen · Indore · With us since Aug 2025

Thirty-four years on the same corner, a queue every Diwali, and not a single photograph online. The son wanted to run ads. We said take one step back — film the 6 AM tray coming out of the kadhai first.

That one reel, cut nine different ways and run inside a four-kilometre radius through the festival fortnight, did more than the ₹40,000 they'd spent on newspaper inserts the year before. We added a WhatsApp order button, a Google profile with a hundred fresh photos, and a gift-box landing page.

3.4×festive counter footfall
812Kreel views in 30 days
₹19cost per WhatsApp order
+61Google reviews

Straight answer

How we count these numbers

Every figure above comes from one of four places: the platform's own reporting (Meta Ads Manager, Google Ads, Google Business Profile insights), the client's booking or billing system, a physical footfall count agreed with the owner, or WhatsApp Business analytics.

Where a result depends on something we don't control — a festival week, a new competitor, a price change — we say so in the monthly report rather than quietly claiming the credit. And when a month goes badly, that goes in the report too.

What we won't do

  • Buy followers, views or engagement
  • Write reviews for you, or pay anyone to
  • Count "impressions" as if they were customers
  • Take a hidden commission on your ad spend
  • Work with your direct competitor in the same locality
  • Hold your accounts hostage if you leave
