Why nobody walks in

Your shop isn't invisible. It's just never been filmed.

The samosas are good. The haircut is good. The trainer actually turns up. None of that travels three streets on its own any more.

What travels is a nine-second vertical video, a Google listing with fresh photos, and an ad that shows up while someone is standing outside deciding where to go. That's the entire job — and it's the part most small businesses never get around to, because running the shop already takes all day.

What "near me" really looks like

Four out of five local searches end in a visit or a call within a day. The listing that shows up first usually wins — not the best shop, just the best-maintained profile.

How it runs

Five steps, and you're only needed for one of them

Most owners give us about ninety minutes in the first fortnight. After that we send work, you approve it on WhatsApp.

01

We come and look

Day 1 — 60 minutes, at your shop

We sit at your counter for an hour. What sells, what doesn't, who walks in, what your busiest hour looks like, what your neighbours are doing. Then we audit whatever you already have online and tell you the honest version.

02

Script, shoot, generate

Days 2–10

A month of content in one go: a real shoot for the hero footage, AI video for volume and variations, and captions written for the way people actually search in your city. You approve a batch on WhatsApp — one thumbs up is enough.

03

Go live, small first

Day 10–14

Ads start deliberately small — usually ₹300–₹500 a day across three or four creatives — so we're learning with your money instead of guessing with it. The Google profile and landing page go up the same week.

04

Cut, keep, repeat

Every week, ongoing

Each Monday we look at what actually brought calls, walk-ins and orders. Losers get switched off, winners get more budget and three new variations. This is the boring part that makes the numbers move.

05

One page, first of the month

Monthly

Not a 40-slide deck. One page: what we spent, what came back, what we're changing next month. If a number went down we say so, and why.
