No surprises

What the fee does not cover

Three things, and we'd rather you read them here than find out in month two.

Ad spend

Billed by Google and Meta directly to your card. We recommend ₹8,000–₹15,000 a month for one shop. We take no percentage of it, ever.

Third-party costs

Domain registration, paid scheduling tools if you want them, influencer fees, or premium stock — all billed at cost, and only ever with your approval first.

Travel beyond 50 km

For shoots outside a 50 km radius we add actual travel cost. No per-day "creative director" charges.
