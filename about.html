About us

We're small on purpose

Nimbus Reach is a compact studio built around one idea: the businesses that hold a neighbourhood together deserve the same quality of marketing as the chains trying to replace them — at a price they can actually pay.

From the founder

Why this exists

My family has run a shop. I've watched what happens when a business that is genuinely good at its craft gets quietly overtaken by one that is merely louder online. It isn't a fair fight, and it has almost nothing to do with who makes the better product.

The agencies who could fix it were quoting ₹80,000 a month and talking about "brand ecosystems". The ones who were affordable posted a stock photo of a burger on a Tuesday and called it social media management. There was nothing sensible in between.

So Nimbus Reach does the boring, unglamorous middle: enough good video to keep a page alive, ads pointed at streets rather than states, a Google listing that doesn't look abandoned, and a monthly report a busy shop owner can read while the tea gets cold. AI lets us produce at a volume that used to be impossible at this price. It doesn't replace turning up with a camera, and we don't pretend otherwise.

If we're a fit, you'll know in the first twenty minutes. If we're not, I'll tell you that too — and usually point you at whatever you should do instead.

How we work

Six rules we don't bend

Most of these exist because we've watched somebody else break them and seen what it did to a client.

One client per category, per locality

We won't run the salon on your street and yours at the same time. If there's a clash we say so before you sign, not after.

Bad months get reported too

If the numbers dipped, that's the first line of the report, with what we think caused it and what changes next.

Your accounts stay yours

Everything is created under your ownership with us added as a manager. Leave whenever you like and nothing breaks.

Replies within one working day

Every client gets a WhatsApp group with the people actually doing the work — not a ticket number.

No bought followers, ever

Fake engagement flatters a report and poisons the algorithm that decides who sees you next. It is never worth it.

We'll tell you to stop spending

When more budget stops producing more customers, we say so. Our fee doesn't move with your ad spend, so there's no reason not to.

Short history

How we got here

2021

One café, one favour

The accidental start

A friend's café was two months from closing. We made nine videos over a weekend and ran ₹6,000 of ads. It's still open, and it's still a client.

2022

Word of mouth, one street at a time

First 20 clients

No sales team, no pitch deck. Shop owners told other shop owners. That's still where most of our work comes from.

2024

AI changed the maths

The shift

Generated video meant fifteen creatives a month became affordable at small-shop budgets. It's the single reason our Starter plan can exist at ₹12,999.

2026

Fourteen cities, six people

Where we are now

Deliberately not scaling into a call centre. We cap the number of clients per person so the work stays the reason people stay.
